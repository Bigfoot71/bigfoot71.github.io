I made this for fun. You move a light through a maze, find the exit, and try not to go dark before you get there, because your range shrinks over time, and if it hits zero, it's over.

Along the way there are orbs. Grab them and they give you a temporary boost. Save them and you can spend them to slow down your decay, or unlock different color themes and tile patterns for the maze walls, nothing gameplay-critical, just a bit of variety.

The loop is pretty simple: go fast, grab orbs, find the exit, repeat.
