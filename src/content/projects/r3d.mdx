r3d is a library that can be seen as a 3D extension for raylib.

raylib is deliberately minimal when it comes to 3D, and r3d fills that gap by providing a complete PBR pipeline, with direct lighting, image-based lighting, soft shadows, all of which are available through both deferred and forward paths for transparency.

It also offers a whole suite of effects such as SSAO, SSR, SSGI, bloom, DoF, volumetric fog and obviously several tonemapping modes, and more.

The API stays as close as possible to raylib's style, so if you're already used to raylib, you won't feel out of place.

There are also several bindings: [Odin](https://github.com/Bigfoot71/r3d-odin), [C3](https://github.com/Bigfoot71/r3d-c3), [C#](https://github.com/graphnode/r3d-cs), and [FreePascal](https://github.com/GuvaCode/ray4laz_r3d).
